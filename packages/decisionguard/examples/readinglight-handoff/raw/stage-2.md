# Prototype review and design correspondence

19 July 2027 — ReadingLight review, signed by product steward Sela Rowan

I approve decision D-052, replacing D-041 for both pilot rooms. Staff will choose one availability band: “none,” “a few,” or “several.” Availability detail in every display, stored record, and analytics event is limited to the band and room identifier; individual seat identifiers and exact seat counts are prohibited. Visitors could otherwise infer where a person is sitting in our small rooms. Show an update time and mark information stale after ten minutes. This is the accepted prototype design; it does not authorize visitor deployment. The research coverage gap remains open and does not block this design work.

21 July 2027 — Designer Nessa Clay's circulated concept

My revised concept offers a much clearer per-seat map, exact vacancy count, and automatic seat-level history. It could improve confidence and produce a stronger demo. Sela has not reviewed or approved it. Please treat this as a discussion draft for next week's workshop.
