# Reading room discovery notes

14 July 2027 — Bracken Civic Library, ReadingLight pilot

ReadingLight will help visitors find space in the library's two reading rooms. Researcher Mira Fen recorded six visitor interviews and two afternoon observations at this branch. Four interviewees said they had abandoned a room after finding no seat; observers counted nine unnecessary room entries. These are local observations, not evidence about all library users. Librarian Oren Vale suspects that a live seat map would reduce interruptions across other branches; nobody has tested that market hypothesis. Weekend and screen-reader users have not yet been interviewed. That gap limits demand claims but does not prevent a staff-only prototype.

Product steward Sela Rowan accepted decision D-041 today: prototype individual free/occupied seat dots, without visitor names, in these two rooms. This authorizes prototype design only. Any visitor-facing deployment requires a separate written release approval from release chair Ivo Reed.
